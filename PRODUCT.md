# Product

## Register
product

## Users
People dictating into Windows applications while working at a desk, using a global shortcut and a small companion window to review and copy their words.

## Product Purpose
Acapella turns speech into text in the focused app. Recording must start predictably, stop deliberately, and leave a readable history. Source: README.md, windows/README.md and the user's reported workflow.

## Brand Personality
Calm, precise, familiar. Preserve the Windows app's existing Sidgrove identity, documented in DesignTokens.cs: DM Sans for interface text, serif headings and restrained lavender-blue accents.

## Anti-references
Avoid gradients on cards, decorative coloured selection fills, crowded controls, clipped cards, and excessively wide prose. The existing design-token documentation records the user's preferences for opaque cards and consistent type.

## Design Principles
- Prioritise recording and readable history over a large promotional header.
- Fit a restored desktop window without losing controls or content.
- Use one vocabulary for navigation, actions and feedback.
- Keep the shortcut's behavior consistent across key order.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Preserve keyboard navigation and visible status text. Let prose wrap and lists scroll at supported window sizes. Do not rely on colour alone for recording state.
